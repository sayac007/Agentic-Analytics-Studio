# Official energy-data reuse policies — source evidence handoff

Review date: 2026-10-24. Intended use: private retrospective econometric research in Agentic Analytics Studio. This document summarizes independently accessed primary policy pages; it is **not** a copy of the complete legal texts, a legal opinion, or a blanket source-specific admission. Read the full policies at the URLs below.

## 1. ECB / ESCB: EXR.M.USD.EUR.SP00.A

Full authoritative policy: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html

Relevant sections: “Policy regarding the reuse of ESCB statistics”; “Common conditions for the reuse of ESCB statistics”; “The following additional rules apply”.

Verified policy: Public ESCB statistics are generally freely reusable for commercial and noncommercial purposes, with source quotation and without modifying official statistics including metadata. This does not grant access to confidential data. Standard-format disclaimers apply. Third-party data require prior permission from their originator. Statistics may be revised or withdrawn; exceptional access restrictions may apply.

Suggested credit: “Source: ECB statistics.”

Source-specific remaining check: Verify EXR series provenance/metadata and that no third-party exception applies to this particular data product. Preserve unmodified source separately from any clearly labelled project-derived analysis.

## 2. U.S. EIA: Europe Brent Spot Price FOB, RBRTE

Full authoritative policy: https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php

Relevant sections: “Public domain and use of EIA content”; “Quoting EIA content and translations”; “Protected materials”.

Verified policy: US government EIA information products, including datasets and databases, are generally public domain and may be used/distributed. The EIA requests acknowledgment including publication date. For translations, credit the translator and link to the source. Third-party contributed/licensed content may have separate copyright; logos and some images are protected and are not freely reusable.

Suggested credit: “Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration, Europe Brent Spot Price FOB (RBRTE), [verified publication date/source vintage].”

Source-specific remaining check: Verify that the uploaded RBRTE file is the intended EIA dataset and no special third-party restriction applies. Do not invent publication date; document retrieval date separately.

## 3. European Commission: Weekly Oil Bulletin Germany diesel

Full official legal notice: https://european-union.europa.eu/legal-notice_en
Official dataset landing page: https://energy.ec.europa.eu/data-and-analysis/weekly-oil-bulletin_en
Official unit convention (separate methodology, not licence): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/1999/566/oj/eng/pdf

Relevant legal notice sections: “Copyright notice” and “Disclaimer”.

Verified policy: Unless otherwise indicated, EU-owned website content is offered under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, requiring appropriate attribution and indication of changes. Different individual notices and third-party rights may override this default. Trademarks, logos and certain other rights are excluded. The legal notice references the Commission Decision of 12 December 2011 on reuse of Commission documents.

The official Weekly Oil Bulletin page identifies the historical workbook “Price developments 2005 onwards (xlsx)”. The uploaded workbook's embedded reproduction notice must be evaluated separately, along with the ownership and any source-specific notices for member-state supplied price data.

Suggested credit, conditional on source-specific review: “Source: European Commission, Directorate-General for Energy, Weekly Oil Bulletin, Germany automotive gas oil, prices including taxes. EUR/litre conversion and calendar-overlap monthly estimates calculated by Agentic Analytics Studio from weekly observations; these are not official monthly means.” Include link, retrieval date and snapshot hash.

Source-specific remaining check: Confirm applicability of EU-owned CC BY 4.0/default policy and workbook-specific reproduction notice to derived private analysis. Keep all 13 incomplete diesel months missing. Do not substitute E10.

## Decision discipline for Codex

- A reviewed general policy is evidence of general terms, **not** automatic admission of a specific series.
- Record separately: private analysis; public redistribution of unchanged raw data; public redistribution of derived data.
- Retain URL, page title, retrieval/review time, evidence type, and limitations. If Codex cannot access the page, label this a human-provided research handoff, not independently inspected by Codex.
- Preserve hash/source/unit/provenance gates and human approval. Do not change frozen protocols, fabricate timestamps, or run econometric models in the rights-review task.
- No complete verbatim copies of publisher policies are embedded here; the full legal texts remain available at the official URLs.
