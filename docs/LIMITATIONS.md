# Limits and interpretation

- **69 months, not 207 independent dates.** Tesla, VW and Rest share one market.
  Their shares sum to one. Negative share correlation does not establish substitution.
- **Registrations are supply and demand together.** Deliveries, fleet mix, model
  changeovers, private eligibility, purchases and application dates differ.
- **Accounting is exact; causes are not.** A market/share decomposition does not
  identify price, image, CEO, launch, subsidy or fuel mechanisms.
- **Observed model failure remains visible.** Primary Tesla innovations retain nonstationarity/serial correlation; VW retains strong autocorrelation. Wider state SDs improve this diagnostic while sometimes worsening three-month forecasts. No model is promoted after viewing these results.
- **No market-potential counterfactual.** The primary September 2026 algebraic baseline is 538,438 versus 88,599 observed BEV and 256,774 total Pkw: compensating state/policy terms make that component unsuitable as a no-subsidy demand scenario.
- **Policy competes with latent trends.** All twelve small synthetic recovery
  intervals included zero. The primary SD 0.03 was selected by the recorded rule,
  with only a small error advantage. It is not a validated true state variance.
- **Prediction is conditional.** Prespecified rolling comparisons use today's
  extraction vintage and reference months, not proven historical release availability.
  Unknown future policy is not supplied. Three-month errors overlap; nine 2026
  targets cannot validate frequentist 95% coverage.
- **Diagnostics can expose failure.** ADF/KPSS have limited power with breaks and
  short series. Innovation ACF, Ljung–Box, outliers and annual variation remain
  visible; no observations or signs are tuned away. MCMC convergence establishes
  computation, not identification or model adequacy.
- **Latent levels are not measured motives.** A smoother uses later data; even a
  conditional filtered series at full-window R is not a historical live estimate.
  No curve is automatically labelled brand image, early-adopter share or social norm.
- **Concrete Stage 2 gaps remain.** China-brand allocation is unresolved for
  66/69 months; exact annual model prices cover 23/80 researched top-ten model-years.
  No complete comparable monthly price history, approved petrol history, electricity/
  charging mix or independently admitted diffusion proxy is invented.
- **Source rights remain specific.** Qualified private diesel/ECB use is not
  public redistribution approval; EIA/Refinitiv rights stay not_verified.
- **Synthetic history stays synthetic.** Task 02's null false positives, coverage
  and sign errors limit its identifiability findings. No universal always-on law,
  calibrated continuous MDE, empirical power or causal Tesla ROI is claimed.
